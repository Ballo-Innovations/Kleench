# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two equal primary audiences operating on the same platform:

**Learners** — Mobile-first users in Zambia and broader Africa seeking financial literacy, business skills, crypto education, and certifications. They earn rewards while they learn (learn-to-earn model). Most access the app on Android devices with variable connectivity.

**Creators / Educators** — Instructors, agents, and live streamers uploading content, running live sessions, and earning from their audience. They need simple upload flows and agent registration.

## Product Purpose

KLeench is a premium African edtech super-app that ties learning to earning. Users watch videos, complete certified courses, pass assessments, and receive verifiable certificates — while earning from engagement. The product must feel as premium and trustworthy as MasterClass but as energetic and mobile-native as a top African fintech.

Success = a learner completes a certified course and shares their certificate with pride.

## Brand Personality

**Ambitious. Energetic. Premium.**

Voice: Direct, motivating, never condescending. Speaks to serious people who are building their futures. Uses uppercase and bold type intentionally — not for decoration but for emphasis. Nigerian/Zambian market sensibility: aspirational without being ostentatious.

Emotional goal: The user should feel capable and elevated after every interaction.

## Anti-references

- Cheap bootcamp sites / low-budget course marketplaces — this must feel premium
- Generic Western edtech (beige Coursera/Udemy corporate blandness) — too cold and impersonal
- Banking/finance apps — not cold navy-and-gold fintech even though financial literacy is a pillar

## Design Principles

1. **Learning feels like earning** — progress, completion, and certification should always feel rewarding, not administrative. Celebrate milestones visually.
2. **Premium without pretension** — the interface should feel expensive but never alienating. Confident typography, deliberate color, no filler decoration.
3. **One visual language** — every screen from course discovery to certificate is one cohesive product. No orphan screens that feel like a different app.
4. **Mobile-first, touch-first** — all tap targets 48×48 minimum, thumb-zone primary actions, scroll patterns that feel native.
5. **Progress is always visible** — users should never wonder where they stand. Progress indicators, status badges, and completion states are first-class citizens everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text)
- Tap targets minimum 48×48px
- Reduced motion support required (animations have static fallbacks)
- Works on low-end Android devices — no heavy WebGL or canvas animations
